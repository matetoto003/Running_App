Useful informations

Tempo

Pace refers to the time it takes a runner to complete one kilometer or mile, typically expressed in minutes per kilometer (min/km). It is a commonly used metric to evaluate running performance and track improvement over time.

Cadence

Cadence is the number of steps a runner takes per minute (spm). A higher cadence, usually around 170-180 spm, is associated with better running efficiency and reduced risk of injury.

VO₂ max

VO₂ max is the maximum amount of oxygen the body can utilize during intense exercise, measured in milliliters per kilogram per minute (ml/kg/min). It is a key indicator of cardiovascular fitness and aerobic endurance.

Heart rate zones

Heart rate zones divide training intensity based on a percentage of maximum heart rate. These typically include:

  • Zone 1 (50-60%): Recovery
  • Zone 2 (60-70%): Endurance
  • Zone 3 (70-80%): Tempo
  • Zone 4 (80-90%): Threshold
  • Zone 5 (90-100%): Max effort

Training within the right zone helps improve performance and prevent overtraining.
